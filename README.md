# Rubens Kaiserman

**ML Engineer & Researcher** · MSc student in Mathematics (IM-UFRJ)

[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/rubenskaiserman)

---

I build generative models for physical systems. At **L'CADAME** I work on probabilistic ocean-dynamics forecasting with diffusion models, in a team of mathematicians, statisticians, oceanographers and engineers.

My background is in probability and analysis, and before research I worked as a software engineer. I care about both the theory behind the models and the engineering that makes them run reliably.

## Current work

- Diffusion models for probabilistic, autoregressive ocean forecasting
- ML systems end to end: data pipelines, training, evaluation, reproducibility
- The math behind generative models: SDEs, score-based diffusion, optimal transport and control

## Background

- **Probability:** measure-theoretic probability, high-dimensional probability, Markov chains, SDEs
- **Analysis:** functional analysis, PDEs, Fourier analysis, complex analysis
- **Geometry & algebra:** differential geometry, advanced linear algebra, spectral graph theory

## Experience

- **ML Engineer & Researcher**, L'CADAME (2025 – present)
- **Data Analyst Intern**, LabMA: Brazilian mortality statistics (2024 – 2025)
- **Software Engineer**, Agência Benext (2022 – 2024)
